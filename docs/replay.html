Replay

Step through all 74 days of a practice scenario. Each day shows what the system believed at its midnight checkpoint, what it chose to do, and what it had seen by then.

The system only sees events that had arrived. Every number comes from running the project’s own offline evaluation, then comparing with the answer key.
